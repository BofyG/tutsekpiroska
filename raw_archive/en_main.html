A special note to a centenary

I am pleased to inform you that we can worthily commemorate Piroska Tutseks birth centenary, the famous mezzo soprano in the 20th century, we can again admire her voice and art, we can read again about her and enjoy a large volume of pictures of her roles.

The portrait just born is like her art and I am grateful to all persons  especially to Dr. Szomolányi Gy. István (Stephen) and to all organizations who helped me in this work, sponsored it and so allowed to write these words and share these values with you.


Budapest, 21st September 2005.

Géza Bogdánfy (the son of P. T.)
